The MX Keys S is an update to Logitech's best productivity keyboard, with the most significant change being a 25% reduction in keystroke noise. The switches have been redesigned to dampen sound at both the bottom-out and the upstroke — the result is a keyboard that types quietly enough to be inoffensive in shared spaces without losing the tactile feedback that makes it genuinely satisfying to type on.

The spherical key cavities are the other thing worth noting. Each key is concave-shaped to cup the fingertip, which reduces lateral slip and means you can sustain a natural typing position for longer without fatigue. It is the kind of detail that sounds minor until you have used a keyboard without it for an afternoon.

Smart Illumination adjusts the backlight based on ambient light levels and activates automatically when a hand approaches the keyboard. It sounds gimmicky but works well in practice — you do not have to think about brightness settings.

The keyboard connects to up to three devices simultaneously via Bluetooth or the Logi Bolt USB receiver. Switching between them is a single keypress. USB-C charging provides battery life that Logitech estimates at ten days with backlighting on, or five months with it off.

Compatible with Windows, macOS, Linux, iPad OS. The key layout includes standard media controls, screenshot shortcuts, and a microphone mute button. At £110 it is not cheap, but it is the keyboard most people who type for a living should probably be using.
