The Jikaro is one of those products that solves a problem you didn't know you had. A ring of stainless steel, sized to wrap around Snow Peak's Pack & Carry Fireplace, it turns an open fire into a genuine gathering surface. Set drinks on it, rest a bowl of food, keep your hands close to the heat without crowding the flame. The setup becomes less utilitarian and more intentional.

The name comes from the Japanese word for stove, and the design reflects that etymological simplicity - there are no moving parts, no fussy assembly, just a clean arc of polished steel that sits at the right height for people arranged around it on low camp chairs or directly on the ground. The stainless construction handles direct proximity to heat without warping, and it wipes clean after an evening's use.

It works best with Snow Peak's own fire pits, though the dimensions suit other compact fireplaces. On a campsite or in a garden, it reads as a considered object rather than camping equipment. The kind of thing that signals you've thought about how evenings outdoors should feel, not just function.

At £325 it's a significant spend for what is, stripped back, a metal ring. But the experience it creates - fire at the centre of a table, conversation happening naturally around it - is worth the investment if outdoor evenings matter to you.
