Sennheiser's Momentum line has always been for people who care about how audio sounds rather than how hardware looks on a spec sheet. The Momentum 4 Wireless is the clearest expression of that position: it competes directly with the Sony XM5 and Bose QC45, but the tuning prioritises accuracy and detail over the kind of bass-forward presentation that tends to impress in a two-minute shop demo and fatigue over a long flight.

The ANC is effective - it handles low-frequency drone well, which is the primary use case for most people. The transparency mode is better than most, which matters when you need to catch a boarding announcement without taking the headphones off. The app gives you five-band parametric EQ if you want to adjust the tuning, though the default is well-judged enough that most people won't touch it.

The headline number is 60 hours of battery life. That is two transatlantic round trips without a charge. The headphones support USB-C and wireless charging both. They fold flat for travel in a fabric case that feels less precious than the hard cases that come with some competitors.

At £199 - down from £249 - the Momentum 4 undercuts the Sony XM5 at a point where the audio quality argument goes Sennheiser's way. For serious listening, these are the ones to buy.
