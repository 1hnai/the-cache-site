Some garments earn their keep. The Universal Works Bakers Jacket has been in continuous production since the brand launched — a direct reference to the workwear worn by co-founder David Keyte's father, a baker who wore the same style every day of his working life.

The cut is deceptively simple: a three-button front, patch pockets at chest and hip, and a clean, boxy silhouette that works over a t-shirt, under a heavier coat, or on its own in mild weather. What lifts it above the average chore coat is the fabric — an exclusive 3/1 twill weave produced for Universal Works that has just enough structure to hold its shape while softening pleasingly with wear.

The Navy colourway is the one to buy. It sits somewhere between workwear and smart-casual, which means it goes with virtually everything: dark denim, olive chinos, grey flannel trousers. The fabric holds the navy dye well without looking too uniform.

At £170, it's a considered purchase rather than an impulse. But this is a jacket that will outlast trends and improve over years of use — exactly the kind of thing worth owning one of.
