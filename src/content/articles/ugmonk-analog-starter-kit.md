Ugmonk's Analog system is a physical alternative to the task app cycle. The starter kit includes a natural walnut card holder, a set of Today/Next/Someday cards, and a pack of blank index cards. Each morning you choose three things. The physical act of moving a card from Today to done is more satisfying than ticking a checkbox. The holder sits on your desk without cluttering it. Available in natural walnut or black walnut. If you find digital task managers create more anxiety than they resolve, this is worth trying.
