The AirPods Pro 2 are the kind of product that quietly becomes indispensable. Not because of any single feature, but because the sum of everything works without friction.

The active noise cancellation is the best available in an earbud form factor. It doesn't just dampen — it removes. Board a train, close the ANC, and you're in a different room. The Transparency mode is equally impressive: when you need to hear the world around you, it passes audio through with a naturalness that older generations couldn't manage.

Adaptive Audio, introduced since launch, does the switching automatically based on your environment. In practice it mostly stays out of the way and makes the right call.

Sound quality is excellent for the price and the form. The low end is controlled, the mids are present, and nothing gets harsh at volume. Spatial audio with dynamic head tracking adds real dimension to film and music recorded with it in mind.

Battery life is six hours per charge with noise cancellation on, with the case delivering another 24. Wireless charging, MagSafe compatible, IP54 rated.

At £249, they're the default recommendation for anyone already in the Apple ecosystem. The fit — the silicone ear tips, the stem length — just works for most ears without fiddling. Sometimes the obvious answer is the right one.
