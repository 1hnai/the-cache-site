Essential Loewe Homme sits at the unfashionable end of fragrance. No loud opening, no singular concept, no moment of drama. It opens with cardamom and a mild citrus, settles into rosemary and cedar, and dries down to vetiver and a clean musk that stays close to the skin for most of a working day. The whole arc is quiet and deliberate.

The bottle is frosted glass with a brushed anodised cap, designed by the same house as the fragrance. The Loewe anagram logo debossed into the base. 100ml at eau de toilette concentration, which is appropriate for a fragrance this close-wearing.

This is the sort of thing you reach for when you want to smell presentable without context. It works equally well in a boardroom and on a Saturday morning. At £115 for 100ml it is, relative to the niche fragrance market, straightforwardly priced for what it delivers.
