Sony's WH-1000XM6 arrives as the definitive benchmark in consumer noise cancellation. The headline figure is 12 microphones, feeding the new HD NC Processor QN3 which adapts to your environment in real time, whether that's a busy commuter train or a droning long-haul flight.

Battery life sits at 30 hours with noise cancellation active, or a very useful 3 hours from a 3-minute charge. The cups fold flat for travel and the headband has been redesigned with a softer, wider cushion for longer sessions.

Sony has also added LE Audio support via Bluetooth 5.3, which brings ultra-low latency useful for video and gaming. Hi-Res Audio Wireless certification means lossless audio over compatible sources. The Mastered by Engineers feature applies real-time processing modelled on professional studio settings.

Available in Black and Platinum Silver, the XM6 is the cleaner, quieter version of an already excellent formula. If you spend time on trains, planes, or open-plan offices, this is where you should start looking.
