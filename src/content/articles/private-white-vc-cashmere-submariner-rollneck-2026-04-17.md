Private White V.C. is one of the last manufacturers making garments of this quality entirely in Manchester. The factory on Cottonopolis Road has been producing outerwear since 1854, and the cashmere pieces reflect the same attention to construction as their waxed cotton and ventile work.

The Submariner Rollneck is named for the fold-over collar that sits close to the jaw — longer than a standard turtleneck, narrower than a chunnel collar. In Cobble Grey, the colour sits in the zone between warm grey and stone, which makes it work against almost anything: dark denim, olive chinos, charcoal trousers. It's a versatile piece precisely because it doesn't try to be.

The cashmere is 2-ply, which means it's light enough for early autumn and late spring but insulates properly through winter with a layer underneath. The weight is substantial without feeling heavy — the textile has structure, it drapes cleanly rather than slumping.

At £595 it's a significant investment for a jumper, and it should be. This is the kind of piece that replaces three lesser options because you reach for it first every time. The stitching is tight, the seams are finished properly, and there are no loose threads at the cuffs — details that distinguish something made with attention from something made to a price point.

Available in a range of colourways. Worth trying in the shop if you're in Manchester.
