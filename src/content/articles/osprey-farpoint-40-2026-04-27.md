The Farpoint 40 occupies a specific and useful niche: large enough for a week away, small enough to avoid checked baggage at most major airlines. At 40 litres it meets IATA carry-on guidelines and fits the overhead bin without negotiation.

The pack opens clamshell-style — the entire front panel unzips flat, so you can actually see and reach everything inside without unpacking the whole bag. A lockable ScratchArmor zip protector on the main compartment gives you peace of mind in transit. The harness and hipbelt stow away behind a zippered panel when you're navigating airports or handing the bag over to hotel staff.

The back panel is ventilated and shaped so it doesn't dig into the lumbar regardless of load. Side compression straps let you cinch down when travelling light. There's a dedicated laptop sleeve, a front organisational panel, and a hydration sleeve that doubles as document storage. Everything has a place.

Osprey offers an All Mighty Guarantee — if anything breaks, they'll repair it or replace it for free, forever. At £155 for a bag that solves most travel problems and lasts indefinitely, it earns its place on the bag shelf.
