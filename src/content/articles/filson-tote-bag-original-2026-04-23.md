Filson's Original Tote has been made in Seattle since the company started outfitting Klondike Gold Rush prospectors in 1897. The details have barely changed because they don't need to.

The bag is cut from Filson's medium-weight Rugged Twill canvas — a tight weave that sheds water without a coating, breathes unlike waxed cloth, and softens to a beautiful worn-in texture after years of use. The handles are thick bridle leather, stitched and riveted at the stress points. There is no zip. There is a press stud. This is intentional: a tote should open quickly and close reliably.

The base dimensions (38 × 30 × 17cm) are capacious enough for a change of clothes, a laptop, and your lunch. The single main compartment keeps life simple. Internal pockets have a way of becoming dumping grounds for things you forget you own.

At £125 it requires some conviction, but the maths works out. A bag you replace every three years at £40 costs the same over a decade as one you keep forever. The Filson is in the forever category. It turns up regularly on eBay as heirloom sales, often for more than it cost new.

Available in navy, tan, otter green, and smoke grey. The navy is the correct answer.
