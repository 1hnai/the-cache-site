The Baggies have been in the Patagonia line since 1982, which is an unusual length of time for a piece of technical clothing to remain essentially unchanged. The design has been refined — the fabric is recycled nylon now, the liner has been improved — but the fundamental proposition hasn't shifted: a lightweight short that dries fast, swims when needed, and works everywhere from a trail to a pub garden.

The 5-inch inseam is the right choice for most people. Long enough to not be conspicuous in a non-beach context, short enough that movement is genuinely unrestricted. The 2.5-inch version exists for those who know what they're getting into.

The mesh liner is why these double as swim trunks without feeling compromised in either direction. It's not an afterthought — it's lightweight, fast-drying, and doesn't bunch. The outer shell is 100% recycled nylon with a DWR finish that sheds light rain and dries from wet in thirty minutes.

Patagonia's repair programme means these are worth buying as a long-term garment rather than a seasonal replacement. The fabric wears well, the hardware is quality, and if something does give out, Worn Wear will fix it. At £65 they're not cheap for shorts, but they're not pretending to be something they're not — they're the version of this product that Patagonia has been building for forty years.

Available in a range of colourways from subdued to emphatic. The subdued ones wear longer.
