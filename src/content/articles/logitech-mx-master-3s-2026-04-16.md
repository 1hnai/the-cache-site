Most computer mice are functional and forgettable. The MX Master 3S is not. Logitech's flagship wireless mouse has been refined across several generations into something notably better than everything around it at this price point.

The headline feature is the electromagnetic MagSpeed scroll wheel. It operates in two modes: a precise click-to-click mode for line-by-line navigation, and a free-spinning mode that covers hundreds of lines in under a second. The wheel switches between them automatically based on scroll speed. Once you have used it for a week, standard scroll wheels feel broken. This is not a small thing — scrolling is something you do several hundred times a day.

The ergonomics are substantial: a deep sculpted thumb rest, a contoured body shaped for a right hand, and a weight that feels solid without fatigue. The horizontal scroll wheel — operated by the thumb — is genuinely useful for working in spreadsheets or wide-format documents.

Connectivity is via Bluetooth or the included USB-C receiver, and the MX Master 3S supports Easy-Switch for moving between up to three devices. Battery life runs to around 70 days on a full charge via USB-C. Logitech's software is well-made and unobtrusive, which is rarer than it should be in this category.

At £99, it is not cheap for a mouse. But this is a tool most people use for eight or more hours a day. The case for buying something this good is straightforward.
