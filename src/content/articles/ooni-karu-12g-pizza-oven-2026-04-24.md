Ooni has done more for home pizza than any other company in recent memory. The Karu 12G is their best all-rounder: a multi-fuel oven that runs on wood, charcoal, or gas (with a separate burner attachment) and hits 500°C in around fifteen minutes.

At that temperature, a Neapolitan-style pizza cooks in sixty seconds. The crust blisters and chars at the edges while the base stays open and airy — something a conventional home oven, capped at 250°C, simply cannot replicate. This isn't marginal improvement territory. It's a completely different outcome.

The 12G is the updated version of the Karu 12 with a redesigned door that retains heat more effectively and a built-in thermometer for the glass window so you can read the floor temperature without lifting the door. The build quality is solid — stainless steel shell, cordierite stone baking board, folding legs for portability.

Setup takes about ten minutes. The learning curve for launching and turning pizzas is real but short. By your third session, you'll be cooking consistently.

Add a good dough recipe, decent flour (Caputo 00 is the standard), and a metal peel, and you'll have everything you need. At £399 for the oven itself, it pays off faster than you'd expect.
