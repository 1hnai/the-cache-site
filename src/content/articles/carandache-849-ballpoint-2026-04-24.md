There are pens you lose, and there are pens you keep for decades. The Caran d'Ache 849 belongs firmly in the second category.

Made in Geneva since 1969, the 849 is a study in restrained Swiss industrial design. The hexagonal aluminium barrel — anodised in matte black — is machined to tight tolerances and sits perfectly between the fingers without any rubber grip or ergonomic concession. It simply works.

What sets the 849 apart from every other ballpoint at this price is the writing experience. The Goliath cartridge delivers consistent, smooth ink with none of the skip or blobbing that plagues cheaper pens. The push-button mechanism has a satisfying click, and the clip is sturdy enough to survive years of pocket wear.

At £22 it's the kind of purchase you'll stop second-guessing the moment you pick it up. Buy the standard matte black. Replace the cartridge when it runs out. Keep it for the rest of your life.

The 849 is proof that genuinely useful things can also be genuinely beautiful — and that some designs don't need improving.
