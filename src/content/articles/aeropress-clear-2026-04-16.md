The AeroPress has always been the coffee brewer that rewards attention. The Clear version makes that process visible - you can watch the extraction happen through the transparent BPA-free plastic body, which satisfies both the curious and the obsessive in equal measure.

The brewing method is a hybrid of pressure and immersion: ground coffee steeps briefly, then a plunger forces water through a paper micro-filter. The result is a clean, concentrated cup with low bitterness and acidity - closer to espresso in character than French press, but more forgiving to brew than any espresso machine you could own.

The AeroPress Clear uses the same core design that's been iterated on since 2005, with a few refinements. The clarity of the body makes it easier to check seal engagement and grind consistency. It's also genuinely beautiful in use - there's something satisfying about watching the puck compress.

It's compact enough to travel with, robust enough to survive a bag, and produces coffee that will convert most people who try it. The recipes are simple, the clean-up is seconds, and the variables are controllable.
