There are shorts designed for specific activities, and then there are Baggies - which are designed for everything and somehow manage it. Patagonia has been making them since the 1980s and the formula hasn't needed much revision: 100% recycled nylon ripstop, built-in mesh liner, elastic waist with a drawcord, and a 5-inch inseam that sits right in the middle of too short and too long.

They dry fast enough to swim in and wear to lunch. The nylon is light enough that you forget you have them on, but the ripstop weave means they handle scrambling, packing, and washing without complaint. The liner eliminates the need for separate swim trunks, which is the real sleight of hand - it turns one garment into two.

The colourways are the other thing. Patagonia has always committed to colour here in a way that most outdoor brands shy away from - block brights, earthy neutrals, a few patterns. A pair in a good colour is the easiest way to make a summer wardrobe look considered without trying.

Pair with a linen shirt or a plain tee. They don't need much.
