Most headphones in this price range want you to believe they're high-end. The Sennheiser HD 560S doesn't bother with the performance — it just is.

Open-back, over-ear, and built around a 38mm driver with a wide soundstage that makes competing closed-backs sound like you're listening in a cupboard. The frequency response is flat and honest — no hyped bass, no rolled-off highs — which means you hear exactly what's on the recording, nothing more, nothing less. Producers use these. Mastering engineers use these. That's the point.

The headband is adjustable, the ear cups are replaceable, and the detachable 3m cable means you're not paying £30 for a wire replacement in two years. At 240g it's light enough for long sessions. The impedance sits at 120 ohms, which is low enough to drive from a laptop or phone while still benefiting from a decent DAC if you have one.

There's a reason these have been in circulation for years without a meaningful redesign. Sennheiser got it right and left it alone. In a market full of products chasing trends, that restraint is worth something.
