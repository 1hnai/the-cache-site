Empirical is a Copenhagen distillery that does things its own way. Founded in 2017 by Lars Williams and Mark Emil Hermansen - both alumni of Noma - the operation is less a traditional distillery and more a fermentation laboratory that happens to make extraordinary spirits.

Helena is one of their most compelling expressions. It's built around a base of pilsner malt and whole dried jasmine flowers, fermented with a saison yeast strain, then aged in Sauternes casks - those French oak barrels previously used for luscious dessert wine from Bordeaux. The result is a spirit that carries the ghost of the wine: honeyed apricot, beeswax, and dried white flowers, undercut by a funky, slightly savoury edge that keeps it from ever becoming sweet.

The 700ml bottle has an ABV of around 40%, and it's best served cold, neat or over a large ice cube. It works beautifully in cocktails too - try it alongside a dry vermouth and a twist of grapefruit peel.

If you've ever wanted to know what a Copenhagen tasting menu in a bottle would taste like, this is your answer. It's unusual, it's excellent, and there's nothing else quite like it.
