The Nano Puff is one of those rare products that does exactly what it says and has barely needed changing since its introduction. Patagonia's most versatile insulated jacket remains a benchmark for the category.

The fill is PrimaLoft Gold Insulation Eco — 65g in the body, 40g in the sleeves — which is synthetic, meaning it keeps working when wet in a way that down doesn't. The shell is a 10-denier ripstop nylon with a DWR finish: light, quiet, and windproof enough to handle a chilly spring morning or a blustery autumn hillside.

What makes it remarkable is the weight-to-warmth ratio. The whole jacket folds down into its own chest pocket and packs to roughly the size of a can of beans. Stuff it in the bottom of a day bag and forget it's there until you need it. You'll need it.

Patagonia's Worn Wear programme means if the jacket ever gets damaged — a snag, a broken zip — they'll repair it. And they use 100% recycled materials throughout: shell, lining, fill, and zips.

This is the men's version in Black (84212). Women's fit available separately. One of those genuinely timeless outdoor purchases you'll make once and keep for years.
