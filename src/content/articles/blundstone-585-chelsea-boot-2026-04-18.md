Blundstone began making boots in Hobart, Tasmania in 1870, originally for the farming and mining workers who needed footwear that could take serious abuse. The 585 is the direct descendant of that utilitarian original - a Chelsea boot format in waxed nubuck, built for wear rather than occasions.

The construction is deceptively simple. Elastic side panels allow the boot to be pulled on and off without laces - a feature borrowed from equestrian riding boots that Blundstone's founders adapted for industrial use. The waxed nubuck upper is water-resistant from the factory and continues to improve with conditioning over time, developing a surface texture particular to each pair. There are woven pull tabs front and back; the rear tab is long enough to grip with cold hands.

What separates the 585 from the dozens of Chelsea boot imitations on the market is what's inside. The SPS (Support Plus System) pairs a Poron XRD® midsole - a material that stiffens under sharp impact and absorbs shock energy - with a contoured EVA footbed that can be removed for orthotics or replacement. This is a boot engineered for people who stand in it all day.

Rustic Brown is the original and best colourway: a mid-tan that darkens with age and conditioning, works with denim and chinos in equal measure, and develops genuine character over years of wear. These are not boots you rotate out of the collection. These are boots you keep.

At £185 they compare favourably to Chelseaboots costing two to three times more. After six months of daily wear, they will look better than the day you bought them.
