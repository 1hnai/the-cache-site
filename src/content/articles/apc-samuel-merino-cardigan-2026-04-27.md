A.P.C. has spent decades proving that restraint is a design philosophy, not a compromise. The Samuel cardigan is a good example of how that ethos plays out in knitwear: slim fit, button-through front, ribbed cuffs and hem, no fuss. The kind of piece that photographs neutrally and wears well for years.

The fine merino wool is soft enough to wear against skin but structured enough to hold its shape over a shirt or under a jacket. A.P.C.'s colour palette here — off-whites, navy, heather greys — means it slots into most wardrobes without requiring anything new around it.

The fit is trimmer than a classic cardigan but not aggressively so. It reads as put-together in a meeting, relaxed enough for a Saturday. At £235 it's priced where you'd expect for this quality of merino and this level of construction — not cheap, but a long-term piece rather than a seasonal one. Buy it in a neutral and wear it constantly.
