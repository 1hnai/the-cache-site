The beyerdynamic DT 770 PRO has been in continuous production since 1985. That's not a heritage story used to justify a premium - it's evidence that the design solved the problem well enough that nothing needed replacing.

These are closed-back, circumaural headphones built for studio monitoring and professional listening. The 80 ohm version is the one most people should buy: it works across a wide range of sources, from a laptop or phone to a dedicated headphone amplifier, and delivers the headphones' full character without needing specialist equipment.

The sound is detailed and accurate with a well-controlled low end that doesn't bleed into the mids. The treble is extended without being sharp - a classic beyerdynamic character that rewards long listening sessions. The velour earpads and over-ear fit are genuinely comfortable for extended use in a way that plenty of more expensive headphones aren't.

The construction is no-nonsense: plastic and aluminium, replaceable earpads and cable, serviced by beyerdynamic directly if anything fails. These are headphones built to be repaired, which means a single pair can last decades.

At £119, the DT 770 PRO represents exceptional value for what it delivers. They've become the default recommendation for a reason.
