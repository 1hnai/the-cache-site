There are kettles that boil water, and there is the Stagg EKG+. Fellow's flagship pour-over kettle has been the benchmark for precision brewing since it launched, and the connected version — the EKG+ — pushes things further still. Variable temperature control down to a single degree, a counterbalanced handle positioned for the slow circular pour, and a built-in brew stopwatch mean the practical case is airtight. But the real reason it earns shelf space is the design.

The gooseneck spout is drawn with the kind of intentional geometry that belongs in a design museum. The base is weighted, the display clean, and the matte black finish weathers daily use without complaint. Fellow clearly understood that a kettle is one of the most-handled objects in any kitchen — it should reward the hand.

The EKG+ pairs with the Fellow app via Bluetooth for preset temperature profiles, useful if you rotate between filter, Aeropress and espresso. But even unplugged from the app it earns its keep: set a temperature, pour with control, make better coffee. At a time when most kitchen equipment is built down to a price, the Stagg EKG+ is built up to a standard.
