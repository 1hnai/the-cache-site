Most body balms sit on the skin longer than you want them to. Rind Concentrate absorbs in a couple of minutes and leaves a dry finish, which makes it genuinely useful rather than just pleasant to own.

The formula is built around mandarin rind, sweet orange, and petitgrain oils. The scent is sharp and citrus-forward, with none of the synthetic warmth you get from lesser formulations. It holds for a few hours and then fades cleanly.

The packaging is the usual Aesop amber glass with a metal lid. It keeps the product stable and looks good on a shelf without being precious about it. The 120ml jar lasts around three months with regular use.

At £48 it is a luxury, but it is one of those purchases that meaningfully improves a daily routine rather than just adding to it.
