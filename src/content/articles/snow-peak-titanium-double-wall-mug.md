Snow Peak was founded in 1958 in Sanjo, a city in Niigata Prefecture that has been producing metalwork since the Edo period. That heritage shows in the Titanium Double Wall Mug - a 449ml vessel made from grade-1 titanium with a double-wall vacuum construction and an anodised colour finish applied over the bare metal.

At around 100 grams the mug is genuinely lightweight, yet it holds temperature well and the double wall means you can hold it comfortably with a hot drink inside. The folding handle locks flat against the body for packing and clicks open to a fixed angle for use. The anodising is food-safe, applied without primers or adhesives - just an electrochemical process that bonds colour directly to the titanium surface.

Snow Peak occupies an unusual position in the outdoor market. Their products are designed with the same seriousness as professional cookware, and their retail footprint is deliberately limited - they have a flagship store in Tokyo and a small number of international stockists. The result is that their equipment rarely appears on sale and holds its value well.

This mug works equally as a desk coffee cup, a camping vessel, or a trail companion. It is the kind of object that earns a permanent place through usefulness rather than novelty. The coloured edition adds a restraint that the natural titanium finish lacks - the specific shade sits somewhere between Herschel grey and slate blue, and it photographs well without trying to.
