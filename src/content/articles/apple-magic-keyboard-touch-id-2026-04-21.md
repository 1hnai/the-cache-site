There are fancier keyboards. There are keyboards with more keys, more switches, more personality. But for anyone who spends most of their day in a Mac ecosystem, Apple's Magic Keyboard with Touch ID is a quietly exceptional piece of kit.

The Touch ID sensor is the headline feature - a fingerprint reader built into the top-right corner that unlocks your Mac and authorises Apple Pay purchases without touching the trackpad. It works instantly, every time, and once you have it you'll miss it on any keyboard that doesn't.

The typing feel is better than it gets credit for. Apple's scissor mechanism delivers a satisfying, precise keystroke with just enough travel - it's not mechanical, but it's not mushy either. The slim aluminium chassis sits flat on a desk and keeps your wrists at a comfortable angle.

Battery life is the other quiet win: a single charge via Lightning lasts months. Not weeks - months. You'll pair it, forget about it, and get on with your work.

It connects over Bluetooth and works with any Mac using Apple Silicon or any Mac with a T2 chip. The British English layout is worth specifying at checkout.

A desk essential, plainly priced.
