Craighill makes desk objects, card cases, and pocket knives with the kind of obsessive finish that makes you want to keep them out on display. The Temple Flashlight is their first electronic product, and they treated it the same way: a machined aluminium column, 1.5 inches wide and 5.6 inches tall, built around a 900-lumen LED with three brightness modes and a 2500mAh battery that charges via USB-C.

The column form isn't just visual. The proportions keep it easy to grip and prop upright on a bedside table or shelf, which is where it spends most of its time looking like something from a modern art shop. Available in silver with a yellow silicone button, or white with ceramic paint.
