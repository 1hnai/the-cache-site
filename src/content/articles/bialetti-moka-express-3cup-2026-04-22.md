Gino Ponti designed the Moka Express in 1933 and, with remarkably few modifications, it is still the right answer. Three chambers, an aluminium body, a rubber seal, and physics do the rest. Pressurised water forces through a bed of ground coffee and fills the upper pot in under five minutes.

The 3-cup size is the ideal daily driver — enough for two strong cups or one generous one. It heats evenly on gas and most electric hobs, cleans in thirty seconds, and will outlast every capsule machine you've ever owned. The octagonal form is so resolved it belongs in a design museum, and several do collect it.

Buy the best Italian ground espresso you can find, pack it loosely, use just-off-the-boil water in the base, and keep the heat low. That's the entire instruction manual.

At under thirty pounds it's also the most honest luxury in the kitchen — no subscription, no pods, no firmware updates. Just coffee, the way it should be.
