The kettle is the piece of brewing kit most people ignore. That's a mistake. Pour-over coffee lives and dies by water temperature and flow rate — and a standard kettle gets neither right. Fellow fixed that.

The Stagg EKG Pro has a counterbalanced gooseneck spout that gives you genuine control over where the water goes and how fast it gets there. The temperature dial runs from 60°C to 100°C in 1° increments, which matters if you're working with light roasts that bruise easily above 94°. There's a 60-minute hold mode, so your water stays ready if you're grinding or faffing. The LCD readout is clear without being cluttered.

Build quality is the other thing. This doesn't feel like kitchen equipment. The matte black finish is uniform, the base is solid, and the handle sits right — weighted enough to feel substantial, light enough to pour with one hand. It looks good on the counter and, more importantly, it works.

If you've already got a good grinder and decent beans, the kettle is almost certainly the weak link in your setup. This is the fix. Pairable with a Fellow Ode or Opus grinder if you want to go all in.
