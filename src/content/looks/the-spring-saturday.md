This look is built around the idea that a good Saturday outfit should work across different contexts without requiring a change. Start with Oliver Spencer's Buffalo Jacket in ecru -- it's structured enough to look deliberate but the material relaxes as the day goes on.

Underneath, Sunspel's classic cotton tee in white. It's the kind of thing that looks better the simpler the rest of the outfit is. Pair with the MHL naval trousers -- a high-rise, wide-cut trouser in soft cotton drill that moves well and holds its shape.

Common Projects Achilles Low in white provides the footwear. It's become a reference point for a reason -- the construction is clean, the profile is low, and it doesn't compete with anything else. The Muji canvas tote in natural finishes the look: functional without being tactical, and neutral enough to go with anything.

The overall palette is ecru, off-white, and white -- a tone that reads as warm rather than stark in spring light. Practical, considered, and put together without looking like it tried.
