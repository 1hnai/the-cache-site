The best mornings are the ones with no agenda. Coffee made slowly, notes written by hand, something to light a candle or clear a head. This is a set of objects that earns its place on a windowsill or a camp table - each one considered, none of them loud. The AeroPress Go Plus is the travel version of the machine that changed how people make filter coffee at home: compact, fast enough to be practical, slow enough to be ritual. Artisan Roast's Guatemalan single-origin gives you something worth the attention. The Field Notes Lucky edition, PDW's titanium lighter, and Lochby's waxed canvas journal holder fill the rest of the table without clutter.
