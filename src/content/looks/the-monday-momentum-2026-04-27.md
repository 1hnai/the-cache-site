Monday doesn't need to feel like a reset from a different life. This look is about dressing with enough intention that the week feels chosen rather than arrived at. An Oxford shirt in a natural or ecru that softens through washes. Relaxed Carhartt WIP trousers that won't crease on the train. Common Projects on feet because they go with everything and improve with age. A Tissot PRX on the wrist — dressed enough for the meeting, honest enough for the rest of the day. The Herschel Little America carries a laptop, a notebook, a water bottle, and the illusion that everything is under control.
