The mid-week edit occupies a particular register — not the weekend's studied relaxation, not Monday's full commitment to looking capable. Thursday has its own logic: the week is nearly done, you've earned some latitude, but there's still a meeting or two to navigate. The OCBD from Gitman anchors the look with quiet authority, cut in their heavier Oxford cloth that holds structure through a long day. Nanamica's wool twill trousers sit at the precise midpoint between tailoring and casualwear — the kind of trouser that reads as smart from across the room and comfortable up close. Engineered Garments layers over everything with their characteristic American workwear-meets-Japanese-precision approach. Grenson's whole cut derby keeps the footwear honest. The Junghans tells the time without announcing itself. The Mismo tote carries everything you actually need and nothing you don't.
