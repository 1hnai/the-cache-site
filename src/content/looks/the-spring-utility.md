Start with Oliver Spencer's Conduit Classic in Nash Blue — a faded, medium-weight cotton shirt that sits between casual and considered. The colour is close to slate, which means it works against olive, navy, and most neutrals without visual noise.

Underneath and below, Universal Works' Aston Pant in light olive twill. It's a mid-rise, straight-cut trouser that isn't trying to be anything other than a reliable garment. The olive reads warm in spring light and connects to the green of the F/CE sacoche without matching it exactly.

The New Balance 1906R provides the footwear. Originally a running shoe from New Balance's archive, it has been revived with mesh panels and a foam midsole that sits higher than the classic 990. The black and white colourway is the most versatile and the least self-conscious.

The Seiko 5 Sports does what a daily-wear watch should: keeps accurate time, tolerates knocks, and reads easily on the wrist without demanding attention. The F/CE sacoche in green nylon carries cards, keys, and a phone without requiring a full bag. The whole look is practical without being workwear, and put-together without being dressed up.
