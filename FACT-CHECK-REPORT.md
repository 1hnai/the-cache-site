### CRITICAL

- **nomos-club-campus-38**: Movement stated as "DUW 3001" (automatic) → corrected to **DUW 4001** (hand-wound manual). Case thickness stated as "9.15mm" → corrected to **8.5mm** per official Nomos specs. Power reserve stated as "43 hours" → corrected to **53 hours** (DUW 4001). Description said "Geneva stripes" → corrected to **Glashütte ribbing** (the Swiss finishing technique Geneva stripes are a different, Swiss-origin treatment; Glashütte watches use Glashütte ribbing). Also corrected in frontmatter description.

- **seiko-prospex-1965-heritage-60th-anniversary**: Beat rate stated as "28,800bph" → corrected to **21,600vph** (confirmed by Seiko and movement databases). Accuracy spec stated as "±15 seconds per day" → corrected to **+25/−15 seconds per day** per Seiko's published specification for the 6R55.

- **barbour-beaufort-jacket**: Beaufort stated as arriving "in 1980" → corrected to **1983**. The 1980 date belongs to the Bedale jacket; the Beaufort was developed 1982–83.

- **brompton-p-line-urban**: Weight stated as "under 7.45 kg" → corrected to **approximately 9.65 kg**. The 7.45 kg figure belongs to the all-titanium **T Line**, not the P Line, which uses a titanium rear frame with a steel mainframe. Also corrected description of frame from "butted titanium throughout" to accurate mixed-material specification.

- **tobermory-1996-29-year-old**: Stated as "Scotland's oldest surviving island distillery" → corrected to "one of Scotland's oldest island distilleries." Bowmore Distillery on Islay was founded in 1779, predating Tobermory's 1798 founding. Also: "requisitioned by the Royal Navy" → softened to "used as a naval canteen during WWII" (sources confirm it served as a canteen and naval store; "requisitioned" implies a formal military takeover which sources do not confirm). "Full fumigation" → changed to "full clean-out" as sources describe wild yeast spore removal but do not use the term fumigation.

- **porsche-911-st**: "Limited to exactly 1,963 units, one for each year of the first 911's development" → corrected to "one for each year since the 911's 1963 debut." The 1,963 figure references the **year 1963**, when the 911 debuted at the Frankfurt IAA; it does not refer to years of development.

- **alfa-romeo-junior-veloce**: Power stated as "240 horsepower" → corrected to **280hp** (production version was upgraded from the initially announced 240hp). WLTP range stated as "248 miles" → corrected to "approximately 210 miles" (248 miles is the standard 156hp Elettrica figure; the Veloce sacrifices range for performance, with WLTP of 207–215 miles per multiple sources). Platform corrected from "STLA Medium" to **e-CMP**.

- **leatherman-free-p4-multitool**: Stated "charges via USB-C (yes, the multitool charges now)" → **removed entirely**. The FREE P4 is a fully mechanical tool with no electronic components or USB-C charging (this may be confused with the FREE T4 or another model). Number of knife blades corrected from "three" to **two** (420HC plain + 420HC serrated). Handle material description "titanium-coated handles" → removed (handles are 420HC stainless steel).

- **remarkable-paper-pro**: Pen-to-ink latency stated as "under one millisecond" → corrected to **approximately 12 milliseconds** per reMarkable's published specifications. Base price corrected from "$629" to **$579** ($629 is the Marker Plus bundle price; the base configuration with standard Marker starts at $579).

- **kestin-crammond-shirt**: Brand founding year stated as "2016" → corrected to **2015** per Kestin's own About page and multiple trade sources.
